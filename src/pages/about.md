TDCC Stories collects practical stories from the life sciences and health research community. They cover how people set up data stewardship programmes, built and ran communities, developed interoperable research software and put research infrastructure in place, and what they learned along the way.

Every story comes with a short set of metadata, such as the story type, the key audience and the organisations involved, so you can quickly see whether it is relevant to you.

## Contributing a story

Stories are written and edited in [Pages CMS](https://app.pagescms.org). New stories start as drafts and only appear on this site once the draft box is unticked.
